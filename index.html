CourtVision

Basketball highlight reels, edited automatically.

Give it a long highlight video, a player and a song. It picks the plays, keeps the player in frame, and cuts a 30-second vertical edit on the beat.

What it's looking at

The Derrick Rose reel with the tracking drawn on. Yellow is Rose, grey is everyone else, orange is the ball and blue is the rim. Whatever's inside the green box makes the final cut.

Four models do the looking, all running on a laptop: one finds the players and the ball, one finds the rim, one notices when the broadcast switches cameras, and one reads jersey numbers so the reel stays on the right player.

How it works

  1. Watch the whole video
  2. Find the plays
  3. Pick the best ones
  4. Cut to the song
  5. Reframe for a phone

About 40,000 lines of Python, run offline. The same video and song always give the same edit. More in METHOD.md and ARCHITECTURE.md.
