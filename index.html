CourtVision

You give it a half-hour highlight video, a player and a song. It picks the plays, follows the player, and cuts a half-minute vertical edit on the beat. No hand edits in anything below.

Six reels

sound on

    What it sees

    Derrick Rose, overlay on
    • everyone on court
    • the player the reel is about
    • …when he slips out of frame
    • the vertical crop that ends up in the reel
    • the ball
    • the rim

    Four models, all running locally on my laptop: one for people and the ball, one for the rim, one that spots broadcast camera cuts, and one that reads jersey numbers so the reel stays on the right guy.

    How it gets there

    ~40k lines of Python
    1. Watch everythingcrowd noise, movement, every camera cut
    2. Find the playswho has the ball, when they shoot, whether it drops
    3. Pick the bestno repeats, nothing that crosses a cut
    4. Cut to the songwhole beats, the big basket on the drop
    5. Reframethe crop follows the player down the court

    It runs offline on one machine, and the same video with the same song gives the same edit, frame for frame. More detail in METHOD.md and ARCHITECTURE.md.
